Post your job offer on the job board of our friends at **BCN Engineering** (BcnEng). BcnEng is a free Slack community of software engineers in Barcelona.

## How to post

1. Join the [BcnEng Slack](https://slack.bcneng.org/).
2. Read their rules: the [netiquette](https://bcneng.org/netiquette/) and the [channel guide](https://bcneng.org/channels/). In short: the job must let people work from Barcelona or near it.
3. Post in `#hiring-job-board` with the Candebot form ([how-to](https://bit.ly/how-to-submit-job-at-bcneng-slack)). The form asks for a salary range.

To get more visibility, [give a talk](/pybcn_association/propose-a-talk/) or host a meetup.
