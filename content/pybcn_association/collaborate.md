PyBCN runs on volunteers. Everything you see here, the monthly meetups, PyDay BCN, PyDataBCN, the PyLadies BCN workshops, is organised by people in the community who decided to help.

There are several ways in, and none of them asks for much to start:

* **[Become a member](/pybcn_association/membership/)** and support the association.
* **[Give a talk](/pybcn_association/propose-a-talk/)** at a meetup. First talks are very welcome, and we will help you prepare it.
* **[Help organise an event](/pybcn_association/organize-with-us/)**, or propose one of your own.
* **[Sponsor us](/pybcn_association/sponsor-us/)**, or offer your office as a venue for a meetup.
* **[Post a job offer](/pybcn_association/job-offers/)** to the community.
* **[Promote your event](/pybcn_association/promote-your-event/)** if it is relevant to Python people in Barcelona.

You can also help with the website: it is [open on GitHub](https://github.com/pybcn/pybcn.github.io), and a correction is as welcome as a feature.

Not sure where you fit? Write to [pybcn@googlegroups.com](mailto:pybcn@googlegroups.com) and tell us what you would enjoy doing.
