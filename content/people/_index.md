Everyone the archive credits, by name. A row holds the roles the person has held, how many events credit them, and the pages that say so: the program of a day, its photos, and the page of the board or the committee they sat on. The tabs above the list keep one role at a time. The board, the committee, and the collaborators of today are grouped on the [Who we are](/pybcn_association/organizers/) page.
