/* TEAM */

    Python Barcelona (PyBCN) is run by its own members. Decisions are made by
    a Management Board and a Permanent Committee. The work is done by work
    groups, and anybody who joins the association can join a work group:

    Meetup            Organizes, prepares and runs the monthly PyBCN meetup.
    PyLadies          Runs the monthly PyLadies BCN workshops and activities.
    Practice Sessions Runs the monthly PyBCN workshops and coding dojos.
    Communication     The channels, the content, and the sponsor contact.
    Web               This site and the process that publishes it.
    Video             Records the talks and publishes them on YouTube.
    Event groups      Formed for one event, such as a PyDay.

    The people are named on their own pages, which stay current:
    Organizers        https://pybcn.org/pybcn_association/organizers/
    PyLadies BCN      https://pybcn.org/pyladies_bcn/organizers-and-speakers/
    Speakers          On the page of each event, https://pybcn.org/events/
