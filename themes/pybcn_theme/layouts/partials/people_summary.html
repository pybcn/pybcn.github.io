{{- /* One person card: the photo, the name, and the role, as one link to the page of the person, /people//, with the social links of the record as a row of icons under it. The card used to open a Bootstrap modal that held the bio, the roles, the events, and the social links of the person. Every person has a page with all of that and more (the talks and the photos) since the Archive gave the person files a URL each (see layouts/people/single.html), and the modal showed a subset of that page: two renderings of one person, only one of them linkable. So the card links to the page. What that removes: the modal markup from every card (22 dialogs on /organizers/: the minified page went from 122702 to 33443 bytes, measured on 2026-10-14), a heading tree inside a dialog that bin/check-rendered had to cut out of the outline of the page, the Bootstrap 4 limit on a modal inside a modal (which is why a photo thumbnail in the modal had to be a link and not a viewer), and the dependency on JavaScript for reading about a person. The page of the person needs none of it. One link, not two. The photo and the name used to be two buttons for one dialog, with the photo at tabindex="-1" so a keyboard user did not stop twice on every card. One anchor around the photo and the text is one tab stop and one target with no trick, and the social links under it stay outside it: an anchor cannot hold another anchor. The alt of the photo is empty, because the name is the text of the same link and an alt that repeats it makes a screen reader say the name twice (axe reports that as image-redundant-alt). The title carries the whole name, because a name too long for the card is cut with an ellipsis (see people.scss) and the hover shows it whole. Photos come from assets/images/people/ and go through the Hugo image pipeline. The grid slot is 350 px wide at most, so one 400 px WebP serves it, with an 800 px variant for a 2x screen. A person with no photo falls back to anon_member.png. image_square.html enlarges a small source to 400 px, where every slot has to be one size, and bin/check-content warns about the photos that are under 400 px. Arguments: person the person page role optional, the role to show instead of pybcn_position: the one the page entry carries, or else the one the heading of the grid gives (see people-grid.html) A remote photo is not supported. The template used to accept photo_url and print it in a plain img, which made every page that lists the person load an image from a third party and tell that party who viewed it.
