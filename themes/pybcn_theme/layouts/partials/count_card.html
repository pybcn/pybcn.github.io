{{- /* The card of counts a row of a listing prints: what that row holds, one icon and one number per kind of thing. [ (comment) 17 (camera) 20 (video) 3 ] Argument: a dict with items, a slice of dicts, each with: kind a key of the vocabulary below: talk, photo, video, links, or event count how many of them the row holds detail optional, a short text that belongs to the count, printed after it in parentheses: the years of the events of a person, "2016 - 2019" The three listings pass their own set, and the card does not know which page it is on. A row of /events/ passes how many talks, photos, and videos the day holds; a row of /talks/ how many photos, videos, and other links the talk has; the Events table of a person page what each talk of the person left behind. The counts come from partials/listing_counts.html, one walk of the site, so the card and the legend above the rows cannot disagree. The items print in the order the caller gives them. An item with a count of zero is left out: a card that reads "0 videos" is noise, and 245 of the 281 rows of /events/ would read it. A row with nothing to count prints no card at all, not an empty box. The icon is the name and the number is the text. Each icon is an image with the noun of its kind as its accessible name and as its hover, the way the type markers of /events/ and /talks/ carry theirs (see event_type_icon.html), so a screen reader hears "Talks 17" and a mouse gets the same word. A bare aria-hidden icon next to a number would leave the reader with "17" and nothing to say what it counts. The noun agrees with the count, "Talk 1" and "Talks 17", because the name is read with the number beside it. The card is a list, so a reader who moves by list item gets one count at a time and can skip the lot. Why Font Awesome and not an emoji. The type of a row is an emoji (data/event_emoji.yaml) and a count is a Font Awesome icon, which is the one split the site keeps: x.com and YouTube have no emoji, so a brand mark has to be Font Awesome, and the counts sit next to those marks. Font Awesome is loaded on every page. The vocabulary. Five kinds, each with an icon and the noun in the singular and the plural. "links" is every link of a talk record that is not its video, under one mark, the chain link of Font Awesome: the slides, the code, a notebook, a site. The kind was "slides" and counted that one type, so a talk with a slides link and a notebooks link said 1 where its page showed 2 (partials/listing_counts.html has the figures). The talk and the photo take their icon from event_types.html, the vocabulary of agenda entry types, by token, so the comment of a talk and the camera of a photo are the glyphs the program grid already draws for them, and a change there reaches here. The nouns are this table's own: the "photo" entry of that table is the group photo slot of a program, and a photo counted here is a picture of a gallery. The other three kinds are not entry types, which is why they are not added to that table: a token of event_types.html is a value a talk's type field may hold, and a talk typed "video" or "event" has to stay the warning partials/event_type_icon.html logs for it.
