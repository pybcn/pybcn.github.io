{{- /* The link to the video of a talk or of a session, with the mark of its host in front. [YouTube mark] Watch on YouTube [YouTube mark] Ready, Set, Go with Numba on YouTube Argument: a dict with url the address of the video title optional. With a title the link reads " on YouTube", which is David Arcos' rule of 2026-10-14 for a video printed on its own: the title of the video is the link to YouTube, with the YouTube mark in front of it. Without one the link reads "Watch on YouTube", for a row that prints the title just above it and would say it twice. blank optional. true opens the video in a new tab, for the block of the programme grid and its modal, which did so before. One partial, so the row of a meetup page, the block of the programme grid, the modal behind it, the page of the talk, and the recording of a whole session print the same link in the same words. The mark is partials/link_mark.html, the one mark per destination the whole site prints, hidden from a screen reader because the words beside it name the host. A host other than YouTube gets the generic mark of that partial and other words: "Watch the video", or "<title> (video)". The host list is the one the Videos block of the gallery read while partials/gallery_content.html built it (its header has the history). Every link of the archive goes to www.youtube.com today, 117 of them, measured on 2026-10-14; the other branch is for the next one. The words say where the link goes before the reader follows it.
