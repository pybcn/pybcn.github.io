We don't track that!

{{ lang.FormatNumberCustom 0 $rsvps.total }} Meetup RSVPs is the sum of the "RSVP: yes" answers on Meetup over the {{ $rsvps.counted }} meetups whose page records the figure, of {{ $rsvps.meetups }}. It is the number the home page prints.

An RSVP is a promise to come, not a seat filled: the people in the room on the day were more or fewer than that, and nobody counted them. The association never recorded who came, so nobody who only attended is named in this archive. It names the people who spoke, organized, mentored, or collaborated, because a program, a board page, or a credit names them.
