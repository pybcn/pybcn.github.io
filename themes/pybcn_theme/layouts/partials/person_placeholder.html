{{- /* The mark of a person with no photo: the initials of the name on a colour derived from the name. In the 48 px slot of a row of /people/, and at 240 px where the portrait of the page of the person would be. [ DA ] on one of ten colours, always the same one for one name Argument: a dict with name the name of the person, as the record writes it (required) This partial is the one place the placeholder lives. The row of /people/ and the header of the page of the person call it and nothing else draws it, so a different placeholder (a pattern drawn by CSS, a figure, a glyph) is a change to this file and to its block of people_index.scss, and to no layout. The size is the caller's: the colours and the letters are the shared rule of people_index.scss, the 48 px of the row sit under .people_rows there, and the 240 px of the page under .person_page in people_page.scss. The page showed nothing where the portrait goes until 2026-10-15, and the placeholder of the row is drawn there now: the initials on a colour. Why initials and a colour. 250 of the 378 records carry no photo. The row used to print the emoji of the first role of the person there (data/role_emoji.yaml), which put 🎤 on some 230 rows in a column: a marker that nearly every row shares marks nothing, and it read as an icon of the site and not as a stand-in for a face. The initials are the one fact about the person the row already has, and they differ from row to row, so a reader who scans the column can tell the rows apart; the colour does the same for a reader who does not read the letters. No image is requested for them, which the anonymous silhouette of the grid (anon_member.webp) would have cost 250 times. The initials. The first letter of the first two words of the name, in upper case: "David Arcos" gives DA and "Àngel Fernández Ibáñez" gives ÀF, the given name and the first surname, which is how a name of this archive is built. A name of one word gives one letter. The letters are cut by rune, not by byte, so an accented initial stays one letter. The colour. One of ten, picked by the FNV-1a hash of the name modulo ten, so one name gets one colour on every build and on every page that may draw it, and two builds cannot disagree. The ten are set in people_index.scss, every one of them at 5:1 or more under white text (WCAG 1.4.3 asks 4.5:1 for text of this size), and the class carries the index, hue_0 to hue_9: no inline style, which bin/check-rendered inspects, and no colour value in the markup.
