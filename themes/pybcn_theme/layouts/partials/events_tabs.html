{{- /* The tabs of the events listing: All, then one tab per series under /events/ that holds an event, each with its count, through partials/filter_tabs.html. [ All 281 ] [ Monthly meetups 268 ] [ PyDay BCN 9 ] [ PyDataBCN 1 ] [ Other events 3 ] Argument: the page being rendered, /events/ or the index of one series, so that its tab is marked current. The tabs are the ones of /talks/ and /people/, with one difference: a view of those two listings is a page a content adapter adds, and a view of this one is a section that already exists, content/events/meetups/, pyday_bcn/, pydatabcn/, and other_events/, each drawn by layouts/events/list.html with the table of /events/ cut to its own events. So the tabs are read from the sections of /events/ and not from a list written here: a fifth series is a folder with an _index.md and gets its tab, and a section that holds no event (no regular page with a year under it, which is the test of a row in partials/event_listing.html) gets none, so a tab never leads to an empty table. The text of a tab is the title of its section page, as the tabs of /talks/ read theirs. The counts are counted here, by the rule of the row, from the same walk of site.RegularPages that partials/listing_counts.html makes for the total of /events/: a page of the events section with a year, filed under its CurrentSection, which for a page of a series is the index of that series. So the count of a tab is the count partials/listing_total.html prints in the h1 of the page it leads to, "Monthly meetups (268)", both from the one test, and the four add up to the All tab (268 + 9 + 1 + 3 = 281 on 2026-10-25). A page of the events section with a year that sits in no series would count in All and in no tab; none does today, and monthly_events.md, the one regular page directly under /events/, has no year and is a row of nothing. The order is the order of the Events menu, the weight of each section page (pyday_bcn 1, pydatabcn 2, other_events 4), with the meetups first: content/events/meetups/_index.md has no menu entry and no weight, which sorts as 0, and its header says why it is kept out of the menu. That is also the tab that holds 268 of the 281 rows, the one a reader needs first, which is the rule of partials/people_tabs.html. Two sections of one weight fall back to their title. No glyph: the legend under the tabs names the types the rows of the page carry (a series is not a type: the meetups hold eleven of them, and other_events/ holds a PyDataBCN edition), so a glyph on the tab would say one type for a page of several. */ -}} {{- $here := . -}} {{- $section := site.GetPage "/events" -}} {{- $counts := dict -}} {{- $total := 0 -}} {{- range $pg := site.RegularPages -}} {{- if ne $pg.Section "events" }}{{ continue }}{{ end -}} {{- if not $pg.Params.year }}{{ continue }}{{ end -}} {{- $total = add $total 1 -}} {{- $key := $pg.CurrentSection.RelPermalink -}} {{- $counts = merge $counts (dict $key (add 1 (int (default 0 (index $counts $key))))) -}} {{- end -}} {{- $items := slice (dict "url" $section.RelPermalink "text" "All" "count" $total "current" (eq $here.RelPermalink $section.RelPermalink)) -}} {{- $series := slice -}} {{- range $section.Sections -}} {{- $count := int (default 0 (index $counts .RelPermalink)) -}} {{- if not $count }}{{ continue }}{{ end -}} {{- $series = $series | append (dict "url" .RelPermalink "text" .Title "count" $count "current" (eq $here.RelPermalink .RelPermalink) "key" (printf "%03d %s" (int .Weight) .Title)) -}} {{- end -}} {{- with $series }}{{ $items = $items | append (sort . "key") }}{{ end -}} {{- partial "filter_tabs.html" (dict "label" "Event series" "items" $items) -}}
