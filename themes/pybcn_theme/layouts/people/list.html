Then the pages that credit the person, each with a marker: an event carries the emoji of its type from data/event_emoji.yaml, the glyph the row of /events/ carries for the same page, and a page with no year (a board page) carries the glyph of the role from data/role_emoji.yaml, the glyph the Roles list of the person page uses. The links are the pages and the person.
